# Risk matrix

| Likelihood \ Impact | Low | Moderate | High | Severe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Almost certain | Medium | High | Critical | Critical |
| Likely | Low/Medium | High | Critical | Critical |
| Possible | Low | Medium | High | Critical |
| Unlikely | Low | Low/Medium | Medium | High |

This is a qualitative prioritization aid, not a formal CVSS calculation. Calibrate labels to the organization's risk appetite and document assumptions.
